Privacy

Your vehicles are visible only to you

Every vehicle, note, picture, and record belongs to your account, and no one else can see or change them. You sign in with your Microsoft account, so there is no extra password to remember.

When you add a record from a file or an email, the file is read only to prepare a draft for you. Nothing becomes part of your garage until you review it and include it, and what you upload is never written to the application's logs.
