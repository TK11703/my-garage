## Validation rules (FluentValidation, one validator per request)

| Request | Rules |
|---|---|
| Fuel or energy | Kind required and defined. Entry date required, 1900-01-01 to today (UTC). Unit price required, 0-9,999.9999, at most 4 decimals. Quantity required, 0.001-99,999.999, at most 3 decimals. Total cost optional, 0-9,999,999.99, at most 2 decimals; when omitted, `round(unit price x quantity, 2)` must not exceed 9,999,999.99. |
| Inspection | Type required and defined. Date required, 1900-01-01 to today (UTC). Cost required, 0-9,999,999.99, at most 2 decimals. Next due date optional; not earlier than the date and not after 2100-12-31. |
| Registration | State required and in `UsStates`. Cost required, 0-9,999,999.99, at most 2 decimals. Expiration date required, 1900-01-01 to 2100-12-31. |
| Insurance | Start date and end date required, each 1900-01-01 to 2100-12-31 (past dates allowed); end date later than start date. Duration required and defined (Monthly, Bi-Annual, or Annual: how often the premium is paid); not cross-checked against the dates. Cost required, 0-9,999,999.99, at most 2 decimals. |
| Tax | Type required and defined. Amount required, 0.01-9,999,999.99, at most 2 decimals. Recurrence required and defined. |

Validation messages name the field in plain words ("Entry date cannot be in the future.", "Unit price can have at most 4 decimal places.") so the Web forms can show them beside the field.

## Relationships and lifecycle

```text
users (Entra id) 1 --- * vehicles 1 --- * fuel_energy_entries
                                    1 --- * inspections
                                    1 --- * registrations
                                    1 --- * insurance_policies
                                    1 --- * taxes
```

- Each entry belongs to one vehicle; deleting a vehicle cascades to all five tables (FR-013, SC-007).
- Entries are created, replaced in full on update, and hard-deleted; there is no state machine and no link between the five kinds or to service records.

## Derived values

| Value | Definition |
|---|---|
| Fuel or energy total | The entered total; or, when left empty, `round(unit_price x quantity, 2)` using `MidpointRounding.AwayFromZero`, computed once on write and stored. |
| Section count | `TotalCount` of the list. |
| Latest fuel or charging date | `entry_date` of the first row of page 1 (newest first). |
